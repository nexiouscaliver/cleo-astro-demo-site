Buyers now ask **ChatGPT**, Gemini and Perplexity for shortlists before they ever open a search box. If your brand is not in those answers, you are not on the list. This guide explains how to **measure** generative engine visibility and what to do with the numbers.

## Why classic rank tracking misses AI answers

Rank trackers report a position on a results page. AI engines return a single synthesised answer, so the useful questions are whether you are *mentioned*, whether you are [cited](https://schema.org/citation), and how prominently.

- **Presence rate** – how often you appear at all
- **Share of voice** – your mentions against competitors
- **Citation rank** – where your pages sit among the sources

![Chart mapping the B2B buying committee](https://cleo-astro-demo-site.vercel.app/images/buying-committee-map.png)

*Map the committee before you map the prompts.*

## Set up a tracking baseline in 3 steps

1. Pick 20–30 prompts your buyers actually ask.
2. Run them daily across at least three engines.
3. Record mentions, citations and sentiment for 30 days.

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Presence rate | Are you in the answer? |
| Sentiment | Is the mention positive? |

> You cannot optimise what you do not measure.
>
> — Peter Drucker (paraphrased)

> **Note:** Engines change weekly: compare 7-day averages, not single days.

### What is GEO visibility?

It is how often, and how prominently, AI answer engines mention and cite your brand for the prompts your buyers ask.
