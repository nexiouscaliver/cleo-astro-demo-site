Choosing a B2B SEO agency is a long-term decision, so the right questions up front save months of rework.

## Do they understand long sales cycles?

B2B buyers research for weeks before they talk to sales. A good agency plans content for every stage, not just bottom-of-funnel keywords.

## How do they measure success?

Rankings alone are not the goal. Ask how the agency ties organic traffic to qualified pipeline and revenue.

## Can they work with your stack?

If your site runs on Astro or another headless setup, make sure the agency can ship changes through your repository and review process.

## What does the first 90 days look like?

Expect a technical audit, a content plan built on real buyer questions, and quick wins on titles, descriptions and internal links.

## How do they handle AI answer engines?

Buyers increasingly shortlist vendors inside AI assistants. Ask how the agency tracks and improves your visibility in those answers.
